- Five-fold mean validation accuracy: 98.3053% +/- 1.0935%.

Conclusion:

- Validation/test statistics were not used to compute normalization parameters.

- 1e-4: best validation accuracy 98.7654%, macro-F1 98.7650%.
- 3e-4: best validation accuracy 100%, macro-F1 100%, loss 0.004633.
- 1e-3: best validation accuracy 100%, macro-F1 100%, loss 0.007641.
- Selected conservative learning rate: 3e-4.

9. METHODS IN THE TRANSLATED DATASET PAPER

The paper compares:
- O-FCOS
- PSC
- O-RTMDet
- O-ATSS
- S2A-Net
- O-FRCNN

Mean performance across the paper's 12 train/test viewing conditions:
- O-RTMDet: Recall 69.92%, Precision 79.11%, F1 72.82%, mAP50 68.28%.
- O-FCOS: Recall 69.28%, Precision 72.42%, F1 70.41%, mAP50 66.57%.
- O-FRCNN: Recall 69.32%, Precision 65.64%, F1 66.69%, mAP50 65.97%.
- O-ATSS: Recall 67.93%, Precision 74.46%, F1 70.29%, mAP50 65.86%.
- PSC: Recall 66.19%, Precision 67.43%, F1 66.19%, mAP50 61.88%.
- S2A-Net: Recall 52.73%, Precision 59.05%, F1 55.08%, mAP50 49.08%.

Paper protocol:
- MMRotate framework, AdamW, base LR 0.001, weight decay 0.05.
- Batch size 64 and 100 training steps/epochs as translated.
- Pretrained backbone initialization.
- Eight-channel input: interleaved real and imaginary parts of HH/VH/HV/VV.
- Separate elevation subsets at 40, 45 and 50 degrees plus the combined set.
- The major published gap is cross-elevation/viewpoint generalization.
