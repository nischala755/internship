Abstract-level finding from indexed sources:
The paper constructs a full-polarimetric sub-terahertz ISAR measurement system in an anechoic chamber, measures three scaled space-target models, performs polarization calibration using calibration objects, verifies calibration using sphere/dihedral targets, proposes a low-sidelobe ISAR imaging method based on dual-dimension reconstruction / dual-ADMM style reconstruction, and releases the TFPSSTD dataset for polarimetric interpretation, enhancement, and target classification.

Likely research gap for our paper:
Existing TFPSSTD work provides calibrated full-polarimetric ISAR data and imaging methodology, but does not establish polarimetric-fusion oriented object detection benchmarks. Our work can fill this by comparing single-pol, fixed Pauli fusion, and learnable polarimetric fusion for OBB detection on Pol-ISARSpaceTarget, then connecting the findings to TFPSSTD full-pol classification/recognition.
